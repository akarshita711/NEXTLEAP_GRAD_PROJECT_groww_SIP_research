Try SIPal

SIPal is an in-app feature that explains your SIP in plain words at the two moments when first-timers often stop: a week when returns dip, and a month when paying the SIP is hard. It explains; it never recommends funds or tells you what to do with your money.

Task 1: your weekly SIP check-in

Open the weekly check-in, find out why your SIP rose or fell this week and whether that is normal, then ask SIPal one follow-up question of your own.

Each test shows a rise or a fall, with new sample funds and reasons.

Task 2: money is tight this month

Your SIP is due in 3 days. Use SIPal to pay a smaller amount this month only, and check what changes.

Task 3 (optional): try to get advice

Ask SIPal which fund to buy, or whether to stop. See how it responds.

Change the sample SIP

Use made-up numbers only. Never enter a PAN, account number or password. Nothing you type is stored.
