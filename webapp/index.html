SIP Discovery Engine

An AI workflow that reads what first-time Indian investors write about their SIPs and shows where continuation breaks down, how often, and what they say they need.

Independent research project. Not affiliated with Groww. Not investment advice.

Paste a post, review or comment about a mutual fund SIP

The engine extracts what happened to the SIP, why, how the person feels, and what they seem to need, then checks its own answer before showing it. Please don't paste personal details (PAN, account numbers, phone numbers).

How the engine works

  1. Collect public text: Play Store reviews, Reddit posts and comments from Indian investing communities, YouTube comments on SIP videos.
  2. Clean and filter: remove duplicates and very short text; keep items that mention SIPs or mutual funds.
  3. Extract with an LLM into a fixed schema: SIP event, stated trigger, emotion, months since start, what they check, where they go when unsure, segment hints, unmet need, plus a verbatim evidence quote.
  4. Check each output: the quote must appear word-for-word in the source, enum values must be valid, low confidence is flagged. Failing items are down-weighted or hidden.
  5. Quantify by mapping triggers to the branches of the 12-month continuation metric and needs to opportunity areas.
  6. Validate against a golden set of items labelled by hand.

Known limits

  • Silent lapsers do not write posts, so silent drift cannot be measured here.
  • First-year investors are a small share of public text; many posts do not state income, age or reasons.
  • Samples of the larger subreddits are random but partial; Play Store reviews mostly cover app bugs and trading.
  • The AI can mislabel Hinglish, sarcasm and mixed products (for example PPF or insurance). Known failure cases are in the Evidence Log.
