# Screener for SIP interviews (Google Form, 12 MCQs)

**Form title:** First-time SIP investors: a 3-minute survey (study on how people stay invested)

**Intro text (paste into form description):**
I'm a product management student researching how first-time investors in India experience their first SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) in mutual funds. This takes ~3 minutes. 
- I will NOT ask for your PAN, bank/account numbers, folio numbers or any portfolio screenshots.
- Answers are used only for this learning project and are never shared with any company.
- This is research, not investment advice or a sales pitch.
- Some respondents may be invited for a 30-minute chat; you can say no, and you can leave your contact on the last question only if you're open to that.

**Optional (not part of the 12 MCQs):** "Open to a 30-minute chat (voice/video, ₹ voucher as thanks if you like)? Share first name + WhatsApp number or email." Short answer, optional.

## How I will use responses
- Qualify: Q1 = Yes, Q2 in 3–18 months, Q3 = Groww (primary) or other app (backup).
- Sampling for the 5–6 interviews: mix across Q4 (continuers vs. paused/reduced/stopped), Q6 (income stability), Q9 (influence). Avoid interviewing only "happy continuers"; reviews under-represent silent droppers, so the interviews must include some.
