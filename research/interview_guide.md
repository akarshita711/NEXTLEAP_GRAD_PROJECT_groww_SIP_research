# Interview guide: first-time SIP investors (30 minutes)

**Goal:** understand, in the investor's own words, what happened between starting a first SIP and today, especially the moments of doubt, and what they did with that doubt. We are NOT testing a solution here. We are validating or challenging what the discovery engine found.

**Who:** people whose first-ever mutual fund SIP started in roughly the last 3 to 18 months (including people who paused, missed instalments, or continued). Aim for 5 to 6 interviews, with at least 2 who wobbled (thought of pausing, reduced, paused or missed an instalment).

**Rules for you as the interviewer**
- Ask about **real past moments**, not opinions. "Tell me about the last time..." beats "Would you...?"
- Never suggest an answer. Never mention funds to buy. Never judge ("good/bad decision").
- Silence is fine. After an answer, wait 3 seconds, then "What happened next?" or "Say more."
- Do NOT collect PAN, account or folio numbers, or screenshots of portfolios. If they offer, say "No need, just describe it."
- Record only with consent. Take notes in the template at the end.

---

## 0. Opening (2 min), read aloud
"Thanks for making time. I'm a product management student studying how people experience their first SIP. There are no right answers and I'm not selling or advising anything. I won't ask for PAN, account numbers or screenshots. I'll take notes, and I'd like to record the audio only for my notes; nothing will be shared with any company, and your name won't appear anywhere. You can skip any question or stop whenever you like. Is that okay?"

## 1. Starting out (4 min)
1. Tell me how you came to start your first SIP. What was going on at the time?
2. Who or what influenced you? (Probe: friend, creator, family, app, own research.)
3. What did you think would happen over the first year? How much money, how fast, what would it look like?
4. How did you decide the amount, and where does that money come from each month? (Salary leftover, side income, family, savings.)

## 2. The first months (5 min)
5. Walk me through the first few weeks. What did you do after it started?
6. How often did you open the app, and what did you look at? What did you feel when you looked?
7. Was there a moment when what you saw was different from what you expected? Tell me about it. (Probe: what number, what screen, what did you think it meant.)

## 3. The moment of doubt (8 min), the core of the interview
8. Have you ever thought about pausing, reducing or stopping? Tell me about the most recent time.
   - What was happening around you that week? (Money, news, a friend, a post.)
   - What exactly did you see or hear that made you think about it?
   - What were you afraid of, or unsure about?
9. What did you do next? Then what? (Follow the chain: did nothing / asked someone / searched / opened the app / changed something.)
10. Who or what did you turn to? What did they tell you? Did it help? What was still unclear after that?
11. If you did nothing: what stopped you from acting, or from continuing with confidence?
12. (Only if relevant) Have you ever missed an instalment or had a payment fail? How did you find out? What did you do?
13. Were you aware you could pause, skip or reduce the SIP? How did you know or not know? Did you try? What happened?

## 4. In the app and outside it (4 min)
14. When something in the app confused or worried you, what was it? (Probe: dates, status, returns numbers, XIRR, charges, taxes, mandate.)
15. Where do you go outside the app when you feel unsure? (YouTube, Reddit, friends, WhatsApp or Telegram groups, an advisor, Google.) What do you look for there?
16. What did you wish the app had told you, and when?

## 5. Today and looking ahead (4 min)
17. Where is your SIP now? Same, changed, stopped?
18. What would make it easier for you to keep going without worrying?
19. If a friend started their first SIP tomorrow, what's the one thing you'd tell them? (This reveals what they wish they'd known.)

## 6. Close (2 min)
20. Is there anything I didn't ask that I should have?
"Thank you. If you're open to it, I may come back in a week or two with something to try and get your reaction. No obligation."

## Probing toolkit
- "Tell me more about that."  - "What happened right before that?"  - "How did that feel?"
- "Can you give me an example?"  - "What did you do instead?"  - "Who else was involved?"
- Avoid: "Don't you think...", "Was it because of the market?", "Would a reminder help?"

## After each interview (10 min)
Write a 5-line summary: (1) trigger of the doubt, (2) what they believed was going to happen, (3) what they did next, (4) what was missing, (5) one sentence on what would have kept them on track.

## Checking the engine against what you hear
After 5 to 6 interviews, tick which engine findings you saw confirmed, contradicted or new:
- Understanding/expectation gap ("is this normal?") is the biggest trigger for first-year investors
- Money/life events with no middle path between "continue" and "stop"
- Failed or unseen instalments and unclear SIP status
- People turn to YouTube, Reddit and friends, not the app
- Silent drift after doubt ("did nothing")
- Anything the engine did not predict
